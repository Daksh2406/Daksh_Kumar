The Beginning

Where I am today

I'm at the beginning. No funding, no team, no finished product — just a laptop, an internet connection, and a stubborn willingness to learn. I spend my days experimenting with code, reading about business, and trying to understand how technology can solve real problems.

Why tech & entrepreneurship

Technology feels like magic you can actually learn. Entrepreneurship is the art of turning that magic into something useful. I'm drawn to both because they let me create, iterate, and grow on my own terms.

The challenge

Starting with limited resources isn't easy. There's no roadmap, no safety net, and plenty of noise. But I've realised that constraints force creativity. Every small win — a working button, a clear concept — feels earned.

What I'm building toward

I'm not trying to build a unicorn overnight. I'm trying to build skills, a mindset, and a body of work that compounds over time. The goal is simple: become capable enough to create opportunities — for myself and maybe for others.

What I Value

Practical learning

I'd rather build something imperfect than plan something perfect. Doing teaches faster than thinking.

Consistency

Small daily steps beat rare bursts of motivation. Showing up is the real skill.

Independent thinking

I try to form my own opinions instead of repeating what everyone else says.

Creativity

Constraints force better ideas. I look for simple solutions to real problems.

Honesty

I don't pretend to be further along than I am. This site is proof of that.

Patience

Real growth takes time. I'm learning to enjoy the process, not just the outcome.

My Mindset

Keep learning

Every day is a chance to know a little more than yesterday. I'd rather be a student forever than pretend to know it all.

Take action

Planning is useful, but doing is what moves the needle. I try to ship imperfect things rather than dream perfect ones.

Learn from mistakes

Errors are data. I've stopped fearing them and started treating them as feedback loops.

Build useful things

I want to create things that solve real problems — even small ones. Usefulness is the ultimate metric.

Stay curious

Curiosity has taken me further than any tutorial. I follow questions, not just answers.

Long-term progress

I'm playing a long game. Small, consistent steps beat sporadic bursts of motivation.

My Vision

These are goals, not guarantees. But writing them down makes them real.

  • Become a capable entrepreneur who builds with both empathy and logic.
  • Develop strong technical skills in web development and AI.
  • Create digital products and businesses that are genuinely useful.
  • Use practical knowledge to create opportunities for myself and others.
  • Grow through real-world experience, not just theory.
