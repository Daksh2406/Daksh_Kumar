How I Learn

Build, don't just watch

Tutorials are starting points. I learn best when I try to build something real and break it.

Document the process

Writing things down helps me understand them. This website is part of that practice.

Ask better questions

The quality of my questions determines the quality of my learning. I'm working on that.

Stay consistent

Small daily progress beats occasional bursts. Showing up matters more than being perfect.

Use real projects

I learn fastest when there's a real goal — a website, a client need, a problem to solve.

Teach to understand

If I can explain it simply, I actually know it. Writing and sharing forces clarity.
